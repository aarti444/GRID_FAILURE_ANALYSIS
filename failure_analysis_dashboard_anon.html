Asset Breakdown — What Is Failing

9 incidents across 5 grids. Batteries and MPPTs account for two-thirds of all asset failures.
Batteries and MPPTs tie at 3 incidents each. Both are driven by environmental stress — heat/dust on MPPTs, moisture on batteries. These are preventable with weatherproofing and seasonal servicing.

Recurring Root Causes — What Is Actually Driving Failures

Root causes that generated multiple failures. Three remain open and will produce failures in 2026 without intervention.
Root Cause Grid(s) Incidents Risk Status
3-way connector / SPD design flaw Grid Alpha 4 Critical 3 separate fire events (Jun, Aug, Sep). PV overproduction stress. Proactive upgrades done at Grid Golf & Grid Foxtrot — Grid Alpha not resolved.
Under-rated fuses at Grid Charlie Grid Charlie 3 Critical Fuses rated only ~10% above operating max. Caused 3 MPPT failures. Fix (30A fuse upgrade) deferred to 2026 — recurrence is certain until executed.
BYD LVL battery commissioning defects Grid Alpha 4 Critical Battery issues revisited Jan, May (×2), Jun. Partially resolved Oct. Root cause not definitively closed — risk of regression.
VSAT single-point-of-failure comms Grid Echo, Grid Delta 5 Medium Grid Echo subscription expired twice in same year. Grid Delta VSAT failed 3× — moisture damage contributed. No comms redundancy on either site.
Moisture / water ingress Grid Delta, Grid Beta 4 Medium Roof leaks, VSAT moisture damage, rain-damaged battery modules. Weatherproofing standard needs tightening at wet-environment sites.
Developer distribution infrastructure Grid Alpha 5 Medium Transformer failures, MV faults, upriser cables — all developer-owned. No direct fix path. Formal escalation protocol with developer is absent.
Inverter overloading (recurring fuse blowouts) Grid Beta, Grid Foxtrot 3 Low–Med Grid Beta L2 & L3 fuses blown in consecutive months. Grid Foxtrot blown twice in 2 days. Pattern suggests sustained distribution overloading.
3 root causes are confirmed to carry into 2026: Grid Charlie fuse under-rating (fix deferred), Grid Alpha 3-way connector fires (not resolved), and BYD LVL batteries (not definitively closed). These are not surprises — they are scheduled recurrences.
